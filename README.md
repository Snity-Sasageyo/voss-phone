# Voss Phone

A small horror mystery website built around a found phone.

You are looking through the phone of Mara Voss, who disappeared from her appartment. The door was locked from inside. No body was found. The phone was still charged, sitting on the kitchen table.

Everything is inside the phone. 

--- 

## What this is

`voss` is a horror piece disguised as a phone interface.

You unlock the device, explore its apps, and price together what happened by reading messages, notes, photos, calendar and settings.

Everything starts normal but not for long lol.

---

## How to play

### Navigation

- Click the on screen keyboard to enter the passcode
- You can also type the numbers on your keyboard
- Use `backspace` to delete digits
- Tap app icons to open apps.
- Use the `home`button inside apps to return to home screen
- Click black ending to restart

### Apps
- Messages
- Photos
- Notes
- Voice Memos
- Calendar
- Settings
- Camera
